Rapid Changes in the Space Industry Demands Revision in Mission Operations Why the “Mission Control Center” Concept is Deprecated Mission operations can be defined as the moment a spacecraft goes into operational status through the end of mission life. Mission operations systems are a crucial part of a successful mission control center. And as society and our technological capabilities evolve, so does the way we run mission control centers.
